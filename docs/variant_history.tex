{\LARGE\bfseries Variants tested before the proposal\par}\vspace{.5ex}
{\normalsize Companion note to \emph{Protocol suggestions for xyz} \,·\, @ymsynn \,·\, November 13, 2026\par}\vspace{1.5ex}

This note keeps the variants we tried and did not keep: other mark formulas, margin rules, liquidation sizes, packages and vault-sale rules. They were scored in earlier runs, on earlier builds of the market-days and of the engine, so their baselines differ from the paper's final run. Under today's rules, on the three earnings crashes, the earlier runs give 819 takeovers, 810 of them below the external price, \$9.82M inherited and a value at takeover of $-\$152$k; the paper's final run gives 819 takeovers, 814 below the external price, \$10.75M inherited and $-\$157.9$k (values at takeover are not the vault's result). Other earlier baselines and the paper's counterparts: stuck feed, \$14.01M inherited (paper: \$15.11M); seven held-out market-days, 106 takeovers and \$1.61M (paper: 103 and \$1.25M); a $-20\%$ wrong price for 5\,min, \$7.03M handed to the vault (paper: \$7.41M). Compare each variant with the ``today'' figure in its own entry. Variants in \S\ref{sec:mark} to \S\ref{sec:liq} were scored before two relayer rules were added to our reconstruction of today's rules on November 8 (the paper, \S A); on the crash days they change no takeover count.

\textbf{Words used here.} Terms in \textcolor[HTML]{2F74D0}{\textbf{blue}} are what a variant changes against the formula it builds on; red terms are removed. \emph{Hold}: while xyz's book and the feed disagree, the center of the discovery bound stays where it was. \emph{Fair sweep}: the vault also sells to every resting bid at or above the oracle (\S\ref{sec:vault}). \emph{Core package}: an earlier candidate, the mark of \S\ref{var:pk} with the cut-back (\S\ref{var:core}). \emph{Own book A, B, C}: three marks built from xyz's own book, without the external price. \emph{Wash}, \emph{wall}: an attacker trading with itself at a wrong price, or posting large bids above the market; \emph{faithful} when the attacker is a real margined account the engine can liquidate. \emph{Restore-to-IM}: the paper's cut-back (change 3). \emph{Normal markets}: the rest of the three crash days, outside regular hours and the crash window (60\,s before the fall to 30\,min after). \emph{Wick}: a brief spike of xyz's book price. \emph{Engine with margined attackers}: replays where the attacker is a real margined account (faithful). \emph{Vault unwind}: how the vault sells what it inherits (\S\ref{sec:vault}). \emph{(locked)}: a margin tier measured at the position's last increase and kept. Every other term is defined in the paper.

\textbf{Today's rules in the earlier run}, on the market-days as first built (the base for the variants below): on the three crashes 819 takeovers, 810 of them already below the external price, \$9.82M inherited, value at takeover $-\$152$k (not the vault's result), because the mark moves at most 0.5\% per update behind an external price that gaps; a wrong price of $-20\%$ for 5\,min hands the vault \$7.03M and fires 390 stops; with a stuck feed the vault inherits \$14.01M; on the seven held-out market-days, 106 takeovers and \$1.61M inherited.

\subsection{Remove the 0.5\% limit per update}\label{var:p1}
\st{sup}{replaced by \S\ref{var:p4d} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
The published prices follow $E$ at once; Hyperliquid's 1\% per update is the only speed limit.
\begin{equation}
O_t = X^1_t = \D_t\big(\chg{E_t}\big),\qquad X^2_t = \D_t\big(\chg{E_t + e_t}\big)
\end{equation}
Removed: \rem{\clip(\cdot,\ O_{t-k}(1\pm c))} and the hull clamp.
\res{Takeovers come in time: 322 of 579 takeovers below the external price instead of 810 of 819, and value when handed to the vault $+\$84$k instead of $-\$152$k (not the vault's result). But the vault inherits more, \$12.72M instead of \$9.82M, because a mark that follows the crash hands it positions it would otherwise never see. With nothing left to slow a false price, a $-5\%$ wrong price for 30\,s hands it \$2.22M and fires 225 stops: in our reconstruction, the 0.5\% limit is the only defense we see in today's rules against a wrong feed.}

\subsection{A 2\% limit per update}\label{var:auto1}
\st{sup}{not kept (no better than \S\ref{var:p1}, one more parameter) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
Today's formula with a wider clamp: $c = \chg{2\%}$.
\res{625 takeovers, 353 of them below the external price (today 810 of 819), \$12.74M inherited, value at takeover $+\$88$k (not the vault's result): no better than \S\ref{var:p1}, with one more parameter.}

\subsection{Mark = external price (reference)}\label{var:auto2}
\st{sup}{reference, not a proposal \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
How much anchoring alone gives: $X^1_t = X^2_t = \chg{E_t}$.
\res{574 takeovers, 321 of them below the external price (today 810 of 819), \$12.91M inherited, value at takeover $+\$87$k (not the vault's result): no better than \S\ref{var:p1}.}

\subsection{External-anchored band}\label{var:auto3}
\st{sup}{not kept (no better than \S\ref{var:p1}, three more parameters) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
Two prices a band apart around a slow premium, so the book picks the mark inside it:
\begin{equation}
X^{1,2}_t = \chg{C_t \mp w},\qquad \chg{C_t = E_t + \clip\big(\ema_{1800\,\text{s}}(\text{mid}-E),\ \pm 0.5\%\big)},\quad w = 0.25\%
\end{equation}
\res{570 takeovers, 318 of them below the external price (today 810 of 819), \$12.63M inherited, value at takeover $+\$84$k (not the vault's result): the same as \S\ref{var:p1}, with three more parameters.}

\subsection{Median of venues}\label{var:auto4}
\st{rej}{rejected (a competitor's price) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
$X^1_t = E_t,\ X^2_t = \chg{\text{Binance}_t}$: the mark is the median of two venues and the xyz book.
\res{The best of the designs that use an outside venue (555 takeovers, 293 of them below the external price, against 819 and 810 today; value at takeover $+\$91$k, against $-\$152$k; not the vault's result). Rejected anyway: we chose to keep a competitor's price out of the mark, so that xyz's price does not depend on another venue.}

\subsection{Own book A, B, C}\label{var:auto5}
\st{rej}{rejected (A, B); C replaced by \S\ref{var:p2} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
The mark from xyz alone: A: $X^1_t = \chg{\overline{I}^{\,\$100\text{k}}_t}$ (time-averaged impact mid), $X^2_t = \chg{\text{VWAP}_{10\,\text{s}}}$. B: A with each step crossing at most \$1M of liquidation prices. C: A, both inputs $= I_t$ once $E$ confirms the move.
\res{A: 549 takeovers, 295 of them below the external price, value at takeover $+\$82$k (not the vault's result); but a \$1M push of xyz's book at the crash moved the mark 0.19\%, against 0.045 to 0.062\% for \S\ref{var:p2}. B: 184 takeovers, 137 of them below the external price, value $+\$54$k (not the vault's result). C: 250 takeovers, only 12 of them below the external price, value $+\$99$k (not the vault's result), but no better than A against pushes.}

\subsection{Median of the external price, xyz trades and xyz's book}\label{var:p2}
\st{sup}{replaced by \S\ref{var:p4} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
$E$ becomes one vote of three; another is xyz's voluntary trades, which a pushed book alone cannot move:
\begin{equation}
X^1_t = \D_t(E_t),\qquad X^2_t = \D_t\big(\chg{P_t}\big)
\end{equation}
Removed: \rem{e_t} and the clamp. Liquidation, backstop and vault fills are excluded from $P_t$.
\res{On the crashes: 550 takeovers, 300 of them below the external price (today 810 of 819), \$12.76M inherited, value at takeover $+\$86$k (not the vault's result). A $-5\%$ wrong price for 30\,s: no takeover and 1 stop. But $-20\%$ for 5\,min hands the vault \$7.09M (today \$7.03M), because the discovery bound follows the bad price. \$1M pushes of xyz's book at the crash moved the mark 0.045 to 0.062\%, against 0.19\% for own book A.}

\subsection{Window variants of \S\ref{var:p2}}\label{var:auto6}
\st{sup}{not kept (a fixed \$1M window could be moved by five \$500k trades) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
$W_t = \chg{\$1\text{M}}$ over 60\,s; or $W_t = \chg{\max(\$250\text{k}, \text{vol}_{60\,\text{s}})}$; or $H = \chg{60\,\text{s}}$.
\res{Same crash results as \S\ref{var:p2} (549 to 551 takeovers, 299 to 300 of them below the external price, value at takeover $+\$86$k; not the vault's result). Measuring xyz's trades over a fixed \$1M let five \$500k trades move the mark 0.36\%; a window of the last 30\,s of trading (Hyperliquid's own cooldown) did not, and was kept.}

\subsection{Anchor the bound on a robust external price}\label{var:p3}
\st{sup}{replaced by \S\ref{var:p4} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
A short wrong price cannot move the bound's center:
\begin{equation}
R_t = \chg{\med(E_{t-N+1},\dots,E_t)},\qquad N = 10\ \ (\text{or } 20 \approx 60\,\text{s})
\end{equation}
\res{Crash results unchanged (550 to 560 takeovers, 300 to 303 of them below the external price, value at takeover $+\$84$ to 86k; not the vault's result). Wrong prices of $-5\%$: no takeover, no stop. $-20\%$ for 30\,s: \$5.28M taken over with $N=10$ and \$2.65M with $N=20$ (today \$5.31M); held 5\,min: \$7.07M (today \$7.03M). A long wrong price still passes.}

\subsection{The band moves only when the feed and xyz agree}\label{var:p4}
\st{sup}{replaced by \S\ref{var:p4b} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
The bound's center follows $E$ only while xyz's trades agree with it within the band itself:
\begin{equation}
R_t = \begin{cases} E_t & \chg{|E_t - P_t| \le d\,E_t}\\ \chg{R_{t-1}} & \text{otherwise (hold)}\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\res{Crash: 550 takeovers, 298 of them below the external price, value at takeover $+\$75$k (not the vault's result), against 819, 811 and $-\$129$k today (earlier run; both measured here against Binance's price, used only as a yardstick). Every wrong price ($-5\%$ or $-20\%$, up to 5\,min): no takeover, no stop, the mark moves at most 0.03\%. Normal markets: 1 liquidation that only this variant makes, 10 that only today's rules make. Weakness: \$50M of wash trades at $+6\%$ froze the band and lifted the mark 4.44\%, which \S\ref{var:p4b} brings to 0.37\%.}

\subsection{The hold also needs xyz's book}\label{var:p4b}
\st{sup}{replaced by \S\ref{var:p4d} \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
A hold needs xyz's trades \emph{and} its book to disagree with the feed, so wash trades alone cannot freeze it:
\begin{equation}
\text{hold} \iff |E_t - P_t| > d\,E_t\ \ \chg{\wedge\ |E_t - I_t| > d\,E_t \text{ on the same side}}
\end{equation}
\res{The \$50M wash now lifts the mark 0.37\% instead of 4.44\%. Wrong prices as in \S\ref{var:p4}.}

\subsection{A hold expires after 150\,s}\label{var:p4c}
\st{rej}{rejected (a long wrong price came through all at once) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
\S\ref{var:p4b}, but after $\chg{150\,\text{s}}$ (trade.xyz's mark time constant) the center jumps back to $E$.
\res{A $-20\%$ wrong price for 5\,min drops the mark 16.2\% and hands the vault \$8.11M, worse than today (\$6.98M in the same run): when the hold expires, the long wrong price comes through all at once.}

\subsection{A hold steps toward the feed}\label{var:p4d}
\st{sup}{replaced by the packages (\S\ref{sec:pkg}) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
\S\ref{var:p4b}; while a hold lasts, every 150\,s the center moves toward $E$ by one band width, so no hold lasts forever:
\begin{equation}
R \leftarrow R + \chg{\clip(E - R,\ \pm d\,R)}\quad\text{every 150\,s while held}
\end{equation}
\res{A $-20\%$ wrong price for 5\,min: nothing is taken over (today \$7.03M). Crash results as \S\ref{var:p2}. SK Hynix on July 27, the largest real ADL: the cascade of that day (in that earlier run, 945 positions and \$31.5M liquidated, then traders auto-deleveraged) does not start.}

\section{Margin variants}\label{sec:margin}
\textbf{Not in the proposal,} because traders would have felt it: lower leverage for large positions (\$7.45M to \$21.2M of extra margin asked of traders, and bypassed by splitting a position across wallets); a leverage cut around earnings (up to \$49.8M of extra margin); a rule that depends on other traders' positions; earlier liquidation for everyone (1.5 to 2.3 times more forced liquidation). All were tested and dropped; the entries follow.

Measured with the \S\ref{var:p4} mark unless an entry says otherwise.

\subsection{Maintenance buffer \texorpdfstring{$\tfrac34$}{3/4}}\label{var:auto7}
\st{sup}{not kept (moves losses to traders as more forced liquidation) \,·\, earlier run}{Hyperliquid}
$\text{MM} = \chg{\tfrac34}\,\text{IM}$ (fixed at $\tfrac12$ today), with the takeover at $\tfrac23$ of the new MM, at $\tfrac23$ of the old one, or $\tfrac12 \to \tfrac34$ with the feed's speed.
\res{With today's mark: \$6.04M inherited (today \$9.82M), but the losses move to traders as 1.5 to 2.3 times more forced liquidation. With the mark of \S\ref{var:p4d}: \$11.2 to 11.8M inherited (without the buffer \$12.76M).}

\subsection{Size tiers}\label{var:auto8}
\st{rej}{rejected (split across wallets) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
Hyperliquid margin tables by notional: tiers (a) $20\times \to \chg{10\times}$ above \$1M; tiers (b) $\chg{10\times}$ above \$500k, $\chg{5\times}$ above \$2M.
\res{Tiers (a): \$3.50M inherited (instead of \$12.76M), but traders would have to post \$7.45M more margin. Tiers (b): \$2.67M inherited, but \$21.2M more margin. Rejected: a trader avoids it by splitting a position across wallets.}

\subsection{Open-interest share tiers}\label{var:auto9}
\st{rej}{rejected (asks traders for \$6.62M more margin) \,·\, earlier run}{Hyperliquid (locked) or relayer (daily)}
Max leverage $\chg{L/2}$ above 5\% of OI, $\chg{L/4}$ above 15\%, measured at the account's last increase, or on the morning's OI each day.
\res{Measured at the last increase, with today's mark: \$2.84M inherited (today \$9.82M). Measured each morning: \$3.49M inherited (instead of \$12.76M), but \$6.62M more margin from traders.}

\subsection{Liquidation-cluster rule}\label{var:auto10}
\st{rej}{rejected (unfair) \,·\, earlier run}{Hyperliquid}
An account's tier comes from its cluster: same-side positions whose liquidation prices lie within $\chg{k = 2\%}$.
\res{\$1.51M inherited (instead of \$12.76M with the mark of \S\ref{var:p4}), but \$10.5M more margin from traders. Rejected: one trader's margin would depend on other traders' positions.}

\subsection{Earnings leverage cuts}\label{var:auto11}
\st{rej}{rejected (singles out earnings) \,·\, earlier run}{relayer}
Max leverage $\chg{\times\tfrac12}$ (or $\chg{\times\tfrac14}$) from 30\,min (or 5\,min) before the earnings close to 1\,h after.

\subsection{Restore-to-margin liquidation}\label{var:auto14}
\st{lead}{kept: change 3 of the proposal \,·\, earlier run}{Hyperliquid}
A large liquidated position is cut back to a target margin each mark update, with no cooldown:
\begin{equation}
q_{\text{sold}} = \chg{\min\{q : \text{equity} \ge T(q_{\text{left}})\}},\quad T = \text{IM at maximum leverage},\ \text{positions} > \$100\text{k}
\end{equation}
In closed form, $\Delta q = (1 - \text{equity}/\text{IM})\,|q|$.
Tested targets: MM, $1.5$\,MM, IM, $3$\,MM; all sizes or $>\$100$k; IM plus a speed-triggered buffer.
\res{Earlier run (final run, the proposal: 583 takeovers, 322 below the external price, \$11.90M, value $+\$63.4$k; not the vault's result). Target IM, positions above \$100k: \$10.98M inherited (instead of \$12.76M). Target MM: \$16.07M. Target IM at every size: \$11.38M.}

\subsection{An earlier candidate: a frozen \texorpdfstring{$E$}{E} is priced like a closed market}\label{sec:lead}
\st{sup}{replaced by the paper's proposal (\S B); its not-live rule is kept \,·\, earlier run}{relayer + Hyperliquid (restore-to-IM)}
