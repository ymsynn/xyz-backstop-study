{\small
$t$: relayer update, about every 3\,s, read lag $k \ge 1$ \quad
$E_t$: external price \quad
$B_t = \med(\text{bid}_t, \text{ask}_t, \text{last}_t)$: xyz book price, computed by Hyperliquid \quad
$O_t$: oracle \quad $M_t$: mark \quad
$A_t$: anchor, $A_0 = E_0$\\
$c = 50$\,bp \quad $d = 1/\text{maxLev}$ \quad $\kappa = 1\%$ \quad $\tau = 30$\,min \quad $\Delta t^* = \min(\Delta t, \tau/10)$ \quad
$\D_t(x) = \clip\big(x,\ E_t(1\pm d)\big)$, \ $\D^R_t$ the same around the held centre $R$ \quad
$e_t$: 150\,s average of $\mathrm{mid} - E$ \quad $V_t$: the feed's vote\\
When the feed is not live the relayer keeps $O_t$ unrounded and publishes it rounded. Feed live: the venue is open and $E_t$ changed in at least one of the last two updates; otherwise not live\\
$P_t$: volume-weighted median of recent voluntary xyz trades \quad
$I_t$: xyz book impact mid at 6{,}000\,USDC \quad
MM $= \tfrac12$ IM at max leverage. \quad
Backstop below $\tfrac23$\,MM and ADL are unchanged in all three.\\[0.6ex]
New proposal: $A_t$ is the vote's average over $\tau$, kept between the vote and the previous mark ($[a,b]$: the range between $a$ and $b$), so it never lags a move the mark has already made. It does not follow a previous mark that xyz's book alone decided, so a push of the book is not carried on after it ends. It is exempt from the discovery bound $\D_t$: the median always lies between $V_t$ and $A_t$.}
