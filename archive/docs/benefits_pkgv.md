## 5. Simple to explain

3 rows: 0 better, 0 same, 0 worse.

| layer | in one sentence |
|---|---|
| mark: today's relayer, oracle unclamped, xyz's price only after a feed failure | The mark follows trade.xyz's external price at once, and xyz's own book can pull it at most today's 50 bp away. | liquidation size: restore to IM | When a large position is liquidated, the engine sells just enough to put it back at its initial margin, instead of 20% now and the rest 30 seconds later. |
| vault unwind: fair sweep | The vault sells a little every few seconds, as today, and also sells right away to anyone bidding at least the fair price. |
