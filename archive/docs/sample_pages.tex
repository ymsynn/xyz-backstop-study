{\color{mute}\small trade.xyz protection \,·\, draft 2026-11-10 \,·\, sample of the page layout\par}\vspace{.5ex}
{\LARGE\bfseries\raggedright Seven changes that keep traders out of ADL and make the backstop vault cheaper to run\par}
\vspace{1.2ex}

When a position on xyz can no longer be closed in the order book, the backstop vault takes it over; when the vault cannot, profitable traders on the other side are closed out against their will (ADL). In 392 days xyz had \$85.2M of ADL. This paper proposes seven changes, six of them in trade.xyz's own hands, that remove most of the cases where this happens, make the vault take positions at a fair price when it must, and size the vault's capital from the positions it actually backs. Traders see no difference on a normal day, and an attacker gains less than under today's rules.

\vspace{1.5ex}
\textbf{Two conditions every change must meet.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=.4ex,topsep=.3ex]
\item \textbf{It must not handicap users.} On a normal day a trader sees no difference: no extra liquidation, no extra stop fired, no leverage cut, no rule that depends on who the account is.
\item \textbf{An attacker must profit less than today.} Each attack is replayed with real, margined accounts that the engine can liquidate like anyone else. Under the proposal the attacker must move the mark less and hurt fewer accounts than under today's rules.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1ex}
\textbf{How we know.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em,itemsep=.4ex,topsep=.3ex]
\item \textbf{One engine for every method.} Today's rules, the proposal and every variant we dropped are replayed through the same engine on the same recorded market data, so a difference in the result comes from the rule and from nothing else.
\item \textbf{Everything can be rebuilt.} Each result comes from a command that anyone with the data can run again, and the same input gives the same output.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1ex}
\textbf{How to read the pages that follow.} One page per idea. The title states the message, the chart shows today (orange) against the proposal (blue), and the box at the foot gives the exact rule and where the full results are.

\begin{exact}[Not shown, on purpose]
The vault's profit or loss in a scenario is never reported: it depends on how the vault sells what it inherits, which a replay of a recorded order book cannot test. Backstop loss is measured at the moment of takeover instead. A competitor's price is never an input of the mark. How market makers would react to the new rules is outside this study, so every result holds the order book as it was recorded.
\end{exact}
